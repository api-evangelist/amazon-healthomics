AWS Compute Optimizer added the CloudWatch metrics VolumeIOPSExceededCheck and VolumeThroughputExceededCheck to its EBS recommendations, showing whether workloads persistently exceeded provisioned capacity. The additional metrics are free for EBS volumes on Nitro instances (excluding standard and Multi-Attach variants).
