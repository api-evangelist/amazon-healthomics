Amazon MSK Provisioned with Express brokers now supports Intelligent Rebalancing on all existing clusters at no additional cost, not just newly provisioned ones. The feature automatically rebalances Kafka infrastructure during scaling and runs roughly 180 times faster than the Standard broker approach.
