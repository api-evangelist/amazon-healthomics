SageMaker AI added observability for production generative AI inference endpoints, with a pre-built CloudWatch dashboard showing throughput latency, hardware utilization, model replica counts, scaling events, and initialization cost breakdowns. It supports OpenTelemetry publishing for consolidated visibility.
