AWS introduced a new Local Zone in Hanoi, Vietnam, among the first Asia Pacific zones featuring S3 and EBS Local Snapshots support to help enterprises meet data residency requirements. The zone supports EC2 instances (C7i, M7i, R7i), S3 One Zone-Infrequent Access, EBS with Local Snapshots, ECS and EKS, networking components, and Application Load Balancers.
