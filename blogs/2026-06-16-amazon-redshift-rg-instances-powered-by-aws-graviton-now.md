Amazon Redshift expanded general availability of Graviton-based RG instances to Africa (Cape Town), Asia Pacific (Bangkok), and Mexico (Central). RG instances deliver up to 4.2x better price-performance, roughly 2.4x faster operations versus RA3, and 30% lower cost per vCPU, with incremental manual snapshot storage now metered by individual data components.
