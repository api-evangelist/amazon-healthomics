Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Base, a fully managed retrieval-augmented generation service, is now generally available. Developers can build production-ready AI agents grounded in organizational data without managing vector stores, data pipelines, and retrieval tooling, as the service handles ingestion, storage optimization, and advanced retrieval.
