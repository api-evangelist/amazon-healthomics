AWS added the all-MiniLM-L12-v2 Sentence Transformers model to SageMaker JumpStart. It encodes text into 384-dimensional vector representations for semantic search, text classification, and sentence similarity, emphasizing efficiency and embedding quality for cost-effective production deployments at scale.
