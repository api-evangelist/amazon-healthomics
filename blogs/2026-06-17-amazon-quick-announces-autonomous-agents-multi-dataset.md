Amazon Quick added autonomous agents, multi-dataset analytics, and a redesigned activity feed. Autonomous agents enable natural-language task delegation with granular permission settings, multi-dataset analytics allows cross-dataset querying via natural language, and the modernized feed offers customized notifications and approval features.
