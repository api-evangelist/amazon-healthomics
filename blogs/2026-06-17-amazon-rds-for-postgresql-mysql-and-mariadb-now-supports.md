Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MariaDB now supports AWS Graviton5 M9g instances, delivering up to 30% better performance and 23% better price-performance versus Graviton4. M9g offers new 24xlarge and 48xlarge sizes with 192 vCPUs, 100Gbps networking, and 72Gbps EBS bandwidth in US East (N. Virginia, Ohio), US West (Oregon), and Europe (Frankfurt).
