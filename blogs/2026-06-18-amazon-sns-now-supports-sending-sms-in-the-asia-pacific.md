Amazon SNS customers in the Asia Pacific (Seoul) region can now send SMS messages to subscribers in over 200 worldwide territories. The fully managed pub/sub service dispatches messages to multiple endpoints, with the Seoul expansion enabling SMS broadcast from that region.
