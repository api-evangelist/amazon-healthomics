EC2 nested virtualization expanded to additional Intel instance types including C7i, R7i, M7i, C8id, R8id, M8id, flex variants, I7i, and X8i. Availability now covers US GovCloud (US-East) and US GovCloud (US-West) alongside commercial regions, enabling KVM or Hyper-V deployment on EC2 instances for emulators and hardware simulation.
