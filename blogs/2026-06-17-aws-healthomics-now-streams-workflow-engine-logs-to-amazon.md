AWS HealthOmics now streams workflow engine logs to Amazon CloudWatch in real time, giving bioinformaticians and workflow developers immediate visibility during processing. Engine logs supply orchestration events, task scheduling details, transmission activities, and detailed error documentation, enabling CloudWatch alarms, dashboards, and tool integration.
